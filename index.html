Controlled Semantic Monitoring Test Policy

Purpose

This public synthetic policy describes how an example service handles its customer audit logs. It contains no real customer data and creates no actual contractual duties. The text exists solely to test whether an external monitoring system can distinguish changed obligations from wording changes.

Scope

The policy applies to audit entries generated by account administration and access control operations. Audit entries include the action category and an approximate timestamp; the example service does not include passwords, payment credentials, or user message contents in these entries.

Retention

The service retains each customer audit log for 30 days after it is created and permanently deletes the log when that retention period ends.

Access

Authorized account administrators may review audit records in the service console. The service limits access to audit records according to the account role; public visitors cannot retrieve those records. Internal troubleshooting procedures are documented separately.

Deletion

After the defined retention period expires, an automated maintenance process removes audit entries from active storage. The policy does not promise instantaneous deletion from encrypted recovery backups. Deletion of customer accounts follows a separate request process.

Exceptions

A documented legal preservation notice may require particular records to be retained beyond the ordinary schedule. Such exceptions must be separately approved and logged. The example service does not apply an exception by default.

Change management

A material change to a retention commitment must be reviewed by the service owner before publication. The objective of this test is to identify that substantive change in a source document, not to guess downstream dependencies that have not been explicitly recorded.
