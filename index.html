Data Retention and Customer Notification Policy

This is a controlled evaluation document for Semantic Drift Engine. It describes a fictional cloud service and does not create obligations for real customers.

Purpose and scope

Example Cloud Service uses operational audit logs to investigate security incidents, verify administrative actions, and support customer compliance reviews. This policy defines the retention period, access restrictions, deletion procedure, and notice requirements for changes that affect customers.

Audit log retention

The service retains operational audit logs for 7 days after they are created. During this period, authorized administrators may retrieve logs to investigate suspected account compromise, diagnose service outages, and review configuration changes. The service deletes expired audit logs after the retention period.

Retention applies to authentication records, administrative actions, and changes to security configuration. Customers may export available records before the retention period expires. The service does not guarantee retrieval after deletion.

Customer notification

Customers receive at least 14 days of advance notice before a material change to audit log retention takes effect. The notice states the previous rule, the new rule, the effective date, and the action customers may need to take. Notices appear in the customer administration portal.

Access and protection

Access to operational audit logs is restricted to authorized personnel with a documented business purpose. Stored records are protected by encryption and access controls. Administrative access is logged for subsequent review.

Document control
